A little something from all of us

For Fatima — the heart of this team

always noticing the little things
the calm in every chaotic week
the standard we all reached for
Dear Fatima,

There's no good way to write this, so we'll just say it plainly: the team won't be the same without you. Not because of what you did — though you did plenty, and did it brilliantly — but because of who you were while you did it.

You were the one who noticed when someone was having a hard day. The one who made time even when you didn't have any. The one whose energy in a room made everyone else's work a little lighter.

We built this little board to hold on to a few of those moments — not the deadlines or the deliverables, but the person behind them. The one we're genuinely going to miss.

Wherever you go next is lucky to have you. We already know that, because we've had the privilege of knowing it for a while now.

With love, your team
Left on your desk
